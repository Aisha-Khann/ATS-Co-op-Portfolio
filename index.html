Aisha’s W25 Work Term Experience at Neatco


Introduction

I, Aisha Khan, worked at Neatco Engineering Services Inc. from January 2025 to April 2025 for the first 4 months of my 8 month co-op term. I am currently still employed there as of May 2025. This website reflects on my experience working at Neatco, what I accomplished, and what I learned during my first 4 months. It highlights the goals I have worked towards and achieved as of yet.


About the Employer

Neatco Engineering Services Inc. is a automation company located in Waterloo, Ontario. They specialize in Automation Machinery Manufacturing, creating products to enhance Artificial Intelligence for the Circular Economy. The company has employed over 50 people and is known for creating cutting-edge, practical AI applications. I had the pleasure to help create the official company website where anyone can feel free to learn more about Neatco. Click the button below!

Visit Neatco's Site

The area of computer science that is related to this company includes programming for training of neural networks, software integration, AI Backend Development, working with computer vision systems, and front-end development for computer vision interfaces.


Job Description

I worked as a Machine Vision Developer where I focused on the areas of machine vision and deep learning. I had the pleasure to assist in programming for training neural networks used in a variety of projects, software integration and front-end development for computer vision interfaces. The work required skills in languages python and java, json, and occasionally html and css-styling, some of which I learned in class and others I picked up on during the job.

Some of my duties included:

  • Development: Research cutting edge tools from multiple libraries for use in AI systems.
  • Programming: Work on updating software tools and interfaces used for current development projects. Update labelling tools to create datasets for artificial intelligence training (through the platform CVAT).
  • Remote Assistance: Assist on-site workers with calibration, debugging, and improvements.
  • AI Backend Development: Work on the development of data harvesting for on-site systems allowing for continual retraining.
  • Work with computer vision systems and make libraries for camera interfaces (such as the Basler Camera products).
  • Communicate with industry partners, suppliers, and customers as required.

    • Goals & Learning Outcomes

    In this section I will share some of my personal goals I had set at the beginning of the work term and how I have expanded my learning in the process of achieving them.


    • Goal 1: COMMUNICATING - Integrative Communication

      • Enhance the ability to effectively communicate complex AI and machine vision concepts with cross-functional teams, ensuring clear understanding among technical and non-technical stakeholders.

      Reflection:

      Throughout my co-op term, I improved my ability to communicate complex technical topics by working with crossfunctional teams on a variety of projects. For example, while working with annotations in Python and JSON for our robot's neural network system through the platform CVAT, my team worked towards understanding and implementing structures that affect training outcomes in for neural networks. After refining certain annotations for weld detection and other material sorting (HDPE, Aluminium, Plastic, etc.) images, we began to see effective communication amongst our AI imaging systems and the neural networks. I also supported the launch of our new website using HTML, CSS styling and JavaScript, requiring coordination with both technical and design teams. Meeting up and discussing media ideas, content ideas, and design changes really assisted in my communication and clear understanding between technical and non technical aspects of project management and completion. By the end of the term, I felt more confident presenting updates and explaining technical processes clearly after expanding my understanding through experience. I've learned how effective communication supports smoother collaboration and ensures that everyone, regardless of technical background, can stay aligned on project goal (most exemplified through our new website launch.)


    • Goal 2: CRITICAL & CREATIVE THINKING - Inquiry & Analysis

      • Develop strong analytical skills to assess and evaluate new AI technologies and approaches for machine vision, ensuring effective integration into Neatco's workflows. This will include critical thinking when coming up with different code in software development to achieve a desired result.

      Reflection:

      This term challenged me to think analytically and creatively across multiple projects. While developing code to assist our robot's neural network using Python and JSON, I had to evaluate various ways to structure annotations and understand how different inputs would affect machine learning results. I also explored different coding techniques for sending out automated mass emails using HTML, Python, and CSS. When designing emails to outreach to our clients, it took a certain level of creativity to lure in positive results and interaction with our marketing attempts. Alongside that, implementing creativity through python-HTML code was quite the challenge, yet a fun opportunity to learn more and see our visions come to life. Adding buttons, banners, media, logos, and links to our emails really helped gain audience attention. We would see positive results and client feedback with colourful elements and animated images and gifs. I even got the chance to work with MailerLite and review feedback through email analytics to note which things worked for us and which didn't moving forward. Each task pushed me to assess what approach would be most efficient or scalable. These experiences strengthened my ability to analyze problems, experiment with new tools, and apply what I learned to real-world AI and machine vision systems. I now feel better equipped to think critically in fast-changing environments and can bring valuable insights when evaluating new technologies.


    • Goal 3: CRITICAL & CREATIVE THINKING - Problem Solving

      • Strengthen problem-solving skills in identifying, diagnosing, and resolving technical challenges related to machine vision software and AI solutions.

      Reflection:

      This co-op gave me several opportunities to grow as a problem solver. Debugging code for the neural network system required me to break down complex errors in Python and understand where issues were rooted in our annotation logic. Launching the company's website involved addressing HTML and JavaScript bugs, ensuring cross-browser compatibility, media to be displayed correctly (such as fit all screen sizes), and optimizing performance under tight deadlines. I also encountered email rendering challenges across platforms when working on the mass emails to outreach to large lists of clientele (formatted in Excel and csv files). In each case, I documented what I tried, what worked, and why-building a mini knowledge base for future use. When I assisted my colleagues in their weld detection motor project, I had the opportunity to write a socket-server code connecting a raspberry pi to a personal PC, and then to the motor, where a code and script ran on the personal PC would signal the motor to complete one full 360 degree clockwise rotation. This project included lots of problem solving, including handling the DM432 stepper driver specifications, such as aligning microsteps to time intervals in order to meet a full rotation at a specific revolution speed. Working on that specific project was interesting and meaningful to my career as I not only got to connect software to hardware, but also got to problem solve with external factors such as a raspberry pi, plugged into a power supply, connected to my program which had to correspond to specific motor specifications. Getting the motor to complete 1 full 360 rotation was a long but rewarding process, filled with a lot of problem solving, as well as trial and error. These experiences boosted my confidence in tackling technical problems creatively and systematically, and I've seen how my problem-solving contributed to smoother project workflows.


    • Goal 4: PERSONAL LEARNING GOAL

      • Develop a deeper understanding of AI-driven machine vision technologies, focusing on neural networks, camera systems, and integration into manufacturing systems. Specifically for the use of waste management.

      Reflection:

      Over the course of this term, I deepened my understanding of AI and machine vision through both self-learning and hands-on experience. Working with Python and JSON annotations gave me insight into how training data directly shapes neural network behavior, while observing how these models are used in robotic systems helped me connect theory to practice. I also learned about system integration while helping with the website and email communication tools, recognizing how different technologies (AI, web development, and automation) can interact in a broader tech stack. Experimenting with the trial and error, seeing how the neural network would respond to the image annotations taken on the basler camera, all assisted towards my understanding of AI and machine vision, alongside camera systems and neural networks. These diverse projects helped reinforce core machine vision concepts and their application to waste management initiatives (through our automation projects such as IntelSight) at Neatco. I'm walking away with a stronger technical foundation and more clarity about how my work contributes to environmental innovation and AI for the circular economy.


    • Goal 5: PROFESSIONAL & ETHICAL BEHAVIOUR - Personal Organization/Time Management

      • Improve personal organization and time management to effectively balance multiple tasks and responsibilities in a fastpaced work environment.

      Reflection:

      Managing time effectively was essential during this work term. Balancing multiple ongoing projects-such as building the company website by the deadline of our DiscoveryX Expo, helping with AI system annotations before client meetings, and creating mass email tools to thank our booth visitors and notify our subscribers of important updates, all taught me how to prioritize tasks and keep track of dependencies. Using a daily logbook helped me visualize my week, break work into smaller tasks, and hold myself accountable. We would have daily stand-up meetings in the morning where my supervisor would delegate tasks and also inquire if there were any areas where any of the students may need assistance or had questions about. After those meetings, working on my tasks throughout the day became an organized procedure, helping me divide the 8 hours in my day efficiently. Alongside daily meetings, we would often have weekly meetings pertaining to specific personal projects, where I got to present my work, gain feedback, ask questions, and leave with more insight on how to complete the project more effectively. With marketing, our weekly CIT meetings really helped me design and code our email outreach effectively, meet deadlines and learn more about python and HTML features, especially importing csv files and linking my code to Outlook. With my website project, we had biweekly meetings to discuss each web page content, images, design, and navigation as well as our visions and goals when compared to our old website. These meetings really helped me get a good idea of which tasks to prioritize and which elements to work on or research to learn more about them before adding them to our page. When timelines shifted or obstacles came up, I learned to communicate early and adjust my plans. Launching the official website in April before our DisoveryX Expo (opportunity for new clients and website traffic) was a tight deadline but we were able to execute it perfectly with our meetings, effective communication and creativity. These strategies helped me meet deadlines without sacrificing quality, and I received positive feedback on my organization from my supervisors and colleagues. I now feel better prepared to manage complex workloads and maintain structure in fast-paced tech environments moving forward in my career.


    During the term, I was able to achieve the above goals alongside facing challenges when working with new software, hardware, experiencing trial and error, tight deadlines and lots of problem-solving. However, these experiences made project completion all the more rewarding. Seeing our efforts come to life with quality, was the best feeling ever, and learning as much as I did along the way most definitely helped me prepare both technically an non-technically for future co-ops and jobs.


    Conclusion

    This term was an important step in my growth as a computer science student and software developer. I improved my technical and soft skills and gained valuable real-world experience. I got to explore programming in Python, with JavaScript, JSON, HTML, CSS, and got to work with a raspberry pi, power supply, stepper driver, and interesting annotation platforms such as CVAT. If someone viewed this website, I’d want them to understand that this was a meaningful and transformative opportunity. I'd highly reccommend Neatco Engineering Services Inc. to any fellow students searching for a beginner-friendly yet meaningful co-op position.
